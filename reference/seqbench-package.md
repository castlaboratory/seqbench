# seqbench: Anytime-Valid Sequential Benchmarking of Algorithms

Sequential, anytime-valid comparison of algorithms on paired losses with
a practical-equivalence margin. Confidence sequences for the mean paired
difference, superiority/equivalence/continue decisions, cost accounting
and auditable trajectories.
